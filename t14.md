### Keyboard Info

This keyboard has no part number. Some searching online suggests it could be from a T14 Gen1.

* Width: 285.7 mm. Key pitch: 19.0 mm (standard spacing)
* Components: 6-row keyboard, TrackPoint, 3 buttons.
* Interface: 1-mm pitch 12-pin FPC for the TrackPoint and the backlight; 0.5-mm pitch 40-pin FPC for the keys and buttons.

### TrackPoint

The trackpoint and the backlight share a 12 wire ribbon cable. The trackpoint uses 8 wires. The pinout is the same as x390: Vcc, DAT, RST, M, R, L, GND, CLK.
